# Professional Services Agreement
Illustrative agreement for document-layout testing only

## 1. Parties
This agreement is entered into by Example Consulting LLC, the Service Provider, and Example Client Inc., the Client. Each party acknowledges the scope and payment conditions below.

## 2. Scope of Services
The Service Provider will support financial reporting, forecast preparation, management review, and related advisory services described in an executed statement of work.
### 2.1 Deliverables
- Monthly management reports and supporting schedules supplied by the agreed reporting date.
- Written commentary on material cash flow forecast changes and outstanding accounting questions.
- A monthly review meeting to discuss open decisions, risks, and the work plan.

## 3. Fees and Payment
Invoices are issued monthly. Amounts are payable within thirty days unless the executed statement of work provides a different schedule.
### 3.1 Expenses
Reasonable project expenses require written preapproval and supporting documentation before reimbursement.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party will protect nonpublic business information received from the other party. The disclosure limitations do not apply to information lawfully available without restriction.

## 5. Records and Cooperation
The Client will provide timely access to the financial records and designated personnel necessary to perform the services. The Service Provider will document material open items.

## 6. Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving thirty days of written notice. Fees for services delivered through the termination date remain payable.

## 7. Limitation of Liability
Neither party is liable for consequential damages arising from a failure to perform under this agreement, subject to applicable law and any signed amendment.

## 8. Governing Law
The governing law and forum shall be specified in the executed agreement. This illustrative text is not intended as legal advice.
